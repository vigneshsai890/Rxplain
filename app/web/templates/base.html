Not medical advice

RxPlain only repeats what is on your prescription and on the official drug label. It does not give medical advice. It cannot tell you if a medicine is right for you. Always follow what your doctor or pharmacist tells you.

If something here does not match your bottle, do not change how you take your medicine. Ask your pharmacist.

In an emergency, call your local emergency number. In the US that is 911.
